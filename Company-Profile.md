# TechNova Solutions

## Company Industry
Technology / Software

## Company Size
Small business — approximately 30 employees

## Location
Ghana

## Company Description
TechNova Solutions is a small technology company that provides web-based software services to small and medium-sized businesses.

## Technology Environment

TechNova Solutions uses:
- Employee laptops
- Company email accounts
- Cloud services
- A public website
- An internal network

## Information Assets

TechNova Solutions stores and manages:
- Customer information
- Employee information
- Business documents
- Financial records
- Login credentials
- Company source code
- Security logs

## Business Scenario

TechNova Solutions has grown to approximately 30 employees and now relies heavily on cloud services and remote work. As the company has expanded, its cybersecurity practices have not developed at the same pace.

Management wants to identify the company's major cybersecurity risks and determine appropriate ways to reduce those risks.
