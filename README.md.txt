# Cybersecurity Risk Assessment — TechNova Solutions

## Overview

This project demonstrates a cybersecurity risk assessment conducted for a fictional small technology company, TechNova Solutions.

The assessment focuses on identifying cybersecurity risks, evaluating their likelihood and potential impact, prioritizing risks, and recommending appropriate risk treatment actions.

## Organization

**Company:** TechNova Solutions  
**Industry:** Technology / Software  
**Size:** Approximately 30 employees  
**Location:** Ghana

TechNova Solutions provides web-based software services to small and medium-sized businesses and relies on cloud services, remote work, company email, employee laptops, and an internal network.

## Project Objectives

The objectives of this assessment are to:

- Identify important information assets
- Identify cybersecurity threats and vulnerabilities
- Assess the likelihood and impact of identified risks
- Prioritize cybersecurity risks
- Recommend appropriate risk treatment measures
- Support continuous cybersecurity risk management

## Methodology

The assessment uses a simple qualitative risk assessment approach based on:

- Likelihood
- Impact
- Overall risk level

Risk levels are categorized as:

- Low
- Medium
- High
- Critical

## Key Risks Identified

The assessment identified several significant cybersecurity risks, including:

- Customer information data breach
- Phishing attacks
- Company email compromise
- Unauthorized access
- Financial fraud
- Malware infections
- Cloud security risks
- Unauthorized access to source code

## Risk Treatment

Recommended treatments include:

- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Least-privilege access
- Security awareness training
- Phishing awareness
- Endpoint protection
- Regular patching
- Cloud permission reviews
- Centralized security logging
- Continuous monitoring
