# TechNova Solutions — Information Assets

## Asset 1: Customer Information

Customer information includes names, contact details, account information, and other data provided by TechNova's customers.

## Asset 2: Employee Information

Employee information includes employee names, contact details, employment records, and other information related to TechNova's staff.

## Asset 3: Business Documents

Business documents include internal reports, contracts, company plans, procedures, and other documents used for business operations.

## Asset 4: Financial Records

Financial records include invoices, payment information, financial reports, and other information related to the company's finances.

## Asset 5: Login Credentials

Login credentials include usernames, passwords, authentication information, and other credentials used to access company systems.

## Asset 6: Company Source Code

Company source code includes software code developed and maintained by TechNova Solutions.

## Asset 7: Security Logs

Security logs contain records of system activity, user activity, authentication events, and potential security events.
