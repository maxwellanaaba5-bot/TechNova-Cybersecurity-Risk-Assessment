# TechNova Solutions — Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this cybersecurity risk assessment is to identify, analyze, and prioritize cybersecurity risks that may affect TechNova Solutions' information assets and business operations.

The assessment will help the organization determine appropriate measures for reducing identified risks to an acceptable level.

## 2. Risk Assessment Approach

The assessment considers three main factors:

- **Threat:** What could cause harm to the organization?
- **Likelihood:** How likely is the threat to occur?
- **Impact:** How serious would the consequences be if the threat occurred?

Risk will be prioritized based on the combination of likelihood and impact.

## 3. Risk Rating

The following rating scale will be used:

### Likelihood

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| Low | The event is unlikely to occur |
| Medium | The event could occur |
| High | The event is likely to occur |

### Impact

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| Low | Limited effect on the organization |
| Medium | Significant effect on the organization |
| High | Serious effect on the organization |

## 4. Risk Level

Risk levels will be determined using the following approach:

- **Low Likelihood + Low Impact = Low Risk**
- **Low Likelihood + Medium Impact = Low/Medium Risk**
- **Low Likelihood + High Impact = Medium Risk**
- **Medium Likelihood + Low Impact = Low/Medium Risk**
- **Medium Likelihood + Medium Impact = Medium Risk**
- **Medium Likelihood + High Impact = High Risk**
- **High Likelihood + Low Impact = Medium Risk**
- **High Likelihood + Medium Impact = High Risk**
- **High Likelihood + High Impact = Critical Risk**

## 5. Assessment Scope

The assessment focuses on the following areas:

- Customer information
- Employee information
- Business documents
- Financial records
- Login credentials
- Company source code
- Security logs
- Employee laptops
- Company email
- Cloud services
- Public website
- Internal network

## 6. Risk Assessment Objective

The objective is to identify the major cybersecurity risks affecting TechNova Solutions, determine their likelihood and potential impact, and recommend appropriate risk treatment measures.

## 7. Risk Treatment

After risks have been assessed, TechNova Solutions will determine appropriate treatment actions.

Possible treatment approaches include:

- Reducing the risk through security controls
- Avoiding the activity creating the risk
- Transferring some or all of the risk
- Accepting the risk when it falls within the organization's risk tolerance

## 8. Continuous Monitoring

Cybersecurity risks can change as the organization, technology, and threat landscape change.

TechNova Solutions should therefore periodically review its risks, update its risk assessments, and monitor whether implemented security controls remain effective.

## Conclusion

The assessment identified several cybersecurity risks that could affect TechNova Solutions' information assets and business operations.

The highest-priority risks were associated with customer information, phishing, email compromise, financial fraud, and cloud access.

The assessment demonstrates that cybersecurity risk management involves more than identifying threats. Organizations must evaluate the likelihood and impact of risks, prioritize them, and select appropriate treatment measures based on their risk tolerance.

Risk management should also be an ongoing process because the organization's technology, business activities, and threat landscape can change over time.
