# TechNova Solutions — Risk Treatment Plan

## Purpose

This risk treatment plan identifies the actions TechNova Solutions should take to reduce identified cybersecurity risks to an acceptable level.

## Risk Treatment Actions

| Risk ID | Risk | Treatment Action | Treatment Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| R001 | Customer information data breach | Implement stronger access controls, MFA, encryption, and regular access reviews | Reduce |
| R002 | Unauthorized access to employee information | Apply role-based access control and review user permissions regularly | Reduce |
| R003 | Business document data leakage | Restrict file-sharing permissions and provide security awareness training | Reduce |
| R004 | Financial fraud | Implement MFA, approval controls, and monitoring of financial activities | Reduce |
| R005 | Phishing attack affecting login credentials | Conduct phishing awareness training, enable MFA, and improve email security controls | Reduce |
| R006 | Unauthorized access to source code | Use role-based access control and restrict repository permissions | Reduce |
| R007 | Security log tampering | Restrict log access and send logs to a centralized monitoring system | Reduce |
| R008 | Malware infection on employee laptops | Deploy endpoint protection, maintain regular patching, and provide security awareness training | Reduce |
| R009 | Company email compromise | Implement MFA, email security controls, phishing awareness training, and suspicious-email reporting | Reduce |
| R010 | Unauthorized access to cloud services | Review cloud permissions, apply least privilege, enable MFA, and monitor cloud activity | Reduce |

## Risk Treatment Priority

The highest-priority risks are:

1. R001 — Customer information data breach
2. R005 — Phishing attack affecting login credentials
3. R009 — Company email compromise
4. R004 — Financial fraud
5. R010 — Unauthorized access to cloud services

These risks should receive priority because of their potential impact on sensitive information, business operations, and organizational security.

## Continuous Review

TechNova Solutions should periodically review the effectiveness of these treatment measures. Risk levels may change as the organization's systems, business activities, and threat environment change.
