\# TechNova Solutions — Risk Matrix



\## Risk Rating Matrix



The risk matrix combines likelihood and impact to determine the overall level of cybersecurity risk.



| Likelihood \\ Impact | Low | Medium | High |

|---|---|---|---|

| \*\*High\*\* | Medium | High | Critical |

| \*\*Medium\*\* | Low/Medium | Medium | High |

| \*\*Low\*\* | Low | Low/Medium | Medium |



\## How to Interpret the Matrix



\- \*\*Low Risk:\*\* Usually requires routine monitoring and existing controls.

\- \*\*Medium Risk:\*\* Should be monitored and addressed through appropriate security controls.

\- \*\*High Risk:\*\* Requires management attention and timely treatment.

\- \*\*Critical Risk:\*\* Requires urgent attention and priority treatment.



\## Application to TechNova Solutions



The risk matrix was used to prioritize the risks identified during the assessment.



For example:



\*\*Phishing affecting login credentials\*\*



\- Likelihood: High

\- Impact: High

\- Overall Risk: Critical



\*\*Security log tampering\*\*



\- Likelihood: Low

\- Impact: High

\- Overall Risk: Medium



This demonstrates how both likelihood and impact influence the organization's risk priorities.
